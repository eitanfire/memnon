Does Aurora Public Schools (Adams-Arapahoe 28J) have an employee assistance program?

Yes. Aurora Public Schools (Adams-Arapahoe 28J) has an employee assistance program. Provider: ComPsych (GuidanceResources). Sessions: 5 counseling sessions per issue, per year. Phone: 1-888-324-2631. Log in: guidanceresources.com, Web ID: APS (also for GuidanceNow app).

Who is covered: All APS employees and household members.

Last verified November 27, 2026. Official sources:

  • aurorak12.org
  • aurorak12.org

Making the most of your sessions

  • Call the number, not just the website: the intake call is where you get matched.
  • Ask how sessions are counted, per issue or per year.
  • Write down what is hardest, what you have tried, and what "better" looks like by the last session.
