Does Thompson School District R2-J have an employee assistance program?

Yes. Thompson School District R2-J has an employee assistance program. Provider: AllOne Health. Sessions: six counseling sessions per year, per incident. Phone: 970-242-9536 or 877-679-1100 (toll-free). Log in: www.allonehealth.com/portal, Company Code: cebt.

Who is covered: All APT, Licensed and Classified employees and their dependents; dependents need not be on the district medical plan (per district staff page).

How sessions are counted is unclear. District staff page: 'six counseling sessions per year, per incident'. 2026-27 CEBT Benefit Guide: '6 free counseling sessions per year for CEBT members and dependents under 26' (no per-incident wording). Ask which applies when you call.

Also listed in the district's guide: The 2026-27 CEBT guide also lists an EAP through The Standard (group life and disability carrier): (888) 293-6948, workhealthlife.com/Standard3.

Making the most of your sessions

  • Call the number, not just the website: the intake call is where you get matched.
  • Ask how sessions are counted, per issue or per year.
  • Write down what is hardest, what you have tried, and what "better" looks like by the last session.
