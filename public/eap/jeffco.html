Does Jeffco Public Schools (Jefferson County R-1) have an employee assistance program?

Yes. Jeffco Public Schools (Jefferson County R-1) has an employee assistance program. Provider: Jeffco Public Schools Employee Assistance Program (district-run; EAP staff provide assessment, brief therapy and referral). Sessions: the district does not publish a limit; ask when you call. Phone: 303-982-0377. Log in: EAP page under Human Resources > Total Rewards on the #TeamJeffco intranet (employee login).

Who is covered: All Jeffco Public Schools employees at no cost; assessment and brief therapy also for family members.

Last verified November 27, 2026. Official sources:

  • jeffcopublicschools.org
  • jeffcopublicschools.org

Making the most of your sessions

  • Call the number, not just the website: the intake call is where you get matched.
  • Ask how sessions are counted, per issue or per year.
  • Write down what is hardest, what you have tried, and what "better" looks like by the last session.
