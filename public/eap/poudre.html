Does Poudre School District R-1 have an employee assistance program?

Yes. Poudre School District R-1 has an employee assistance program. Provider: Employee Assistance Services (EAS), run by the district (in-house program). Sessions: five sessions per problem, per plan year. Phone: 970-488-4925; 24/7 crisis response at 970-488-4925, ext. 1. Log in: eas@psdschools.org; https://www.psdschools.org/eas.

Who is covered: PSD employees and eligible household family members; PSD health insurance not required. Temporary employees, substitutes, and varied-hour employees without a pay assignment are not eligible.

Making the most of your sessions

  • Call the number, not just the website: the intake call is where you get matched.
  • Ask how sessions are counted, per issue or per year.
  • Write down what is hardest, what you have tried, and what "better" looks like by the last session.
