Does Weld RE-4 School District (Windsor-Severance) have an employee assistance program?

Yes. Weld RE-4 School District (Windsor-Severance) has an employee assistance program. Provider: AllOne Health (through CEBT). Sessions: 6 free counseling sessions per year. Phone: (970) 242-9536 or (877) 679-1100. Log in: allonehealth.com/portal, Company Code: CEBT.

Who is covered: CEBT members and dependents under 26.

Some of this comes from a past-year or benefits-trust guide, so confirm it with HR.

Also listed in the district's guide: Modern Health: 8 therapy and 8 coaching sessions per year for members and dependents ages 6+ enrolled in a medical plan.

Last verified November 27, 2026. Official sources:

  • weldre4.org
  • cebt.org (PDF)

Making the most of your sessions

  • Call the number, not just the website: the intake call is where you get matched.
  • Ask how sessions are counted, per issue or per year.
  • Write down what is hardest, what you have tried, and what "better" looks like by the last session.
