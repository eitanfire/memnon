Does Adams 12 Five Star Schools have an employee assistance program?

Yes. Adams 12 Five Star Schools has an employee assistance program. Provider: ComPsych (GuidanceResources). Sessions: the district does not publish a limit; ask when you call. Phone: 855-890-3209 (TRS: dial 711), 24/7. Log in: guidanceresources.com, Web ID: ADAMS 12; GuidanceNow app.

Who is covered: All employees and their household members, regardless of benefits eligibility or hours worked.

Last verified November 27, 2026. Official sources:

  • resources.finalsite.net (PDF)
  • adams12.org

Making the most of your sessions

  • Call the number, not just the website: the intake call is where you get matched.
  • Ask how sessions are counted, per issue or per year.
  • Write down what is hardest, what you have tried, and what "better" looks like by the last session.
