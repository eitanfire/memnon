Does St. Vrain Valley School District RE-1J have an employee assistance program?

Yes. St. Vrain Valley School District RE-1J has an employee assistance program. Provider: ComPsych (GuidanceResources). Sessions: up to 6 free face-to-face visits per episode. Phone: 855-699-6908 (24/7). Log in: www.guidanceresources.com, Company Web ID: SVVSD.

Who is covered: Employees and eligible family members; counseling available to all members of the household.

How sessions are counted is unclear. District EAP page and 2026 New Hire Benefits Guide say 'per episode'; the 2024-2025 Classified Employees' Handbook (sec. 3.3) says 'Employees are offered six free visits per year.' Ask which applies when you call.

Also listed in the district's guide: Employees covered on the district Basic Life Plan (The Hartford) get Ability Assist Counseling (also run by ComPsych): up to 3 face-to-face emotional or work-life sessions per occurrence, per year. 1-800-964-3577, Web ID HLF902, Company Name ABILI. Guide says use the district EAP first.

Making the most of your sessions

  • Call the number, not just the website: the intake call is where you get matched.
  • Ask how sessions are counted, per issue or per year.
  • Write down what is hardest, what you have tried, and what "better" looks like by the last session.
