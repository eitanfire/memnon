Does Westminster Public Schools have an employee assistance program?

Yes. Westminster Public Schools has an employee assistance program. Provider: Resources For Living. Sessions: up to five free face-to-face visits per person, per issue, per year. Phone: 866-252-4468. Log in: resourcesforliving.com (also MyLifeValues.com). Login details are in your district's benefits guide.

Who is covered: Employees and their household.

Login details are in your district's benefits guide.

Making the most of your sessions

  • Call the number, not just the website: the intake call is where you get matched.
  • Ask how sessions are counted, per issue or per year.
  • Write down what is hardest, what you have tried, and what "better" looks like by the last session.
