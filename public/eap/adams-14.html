Does Adams County School District 14 have an employee assistance program?

Yes. Adams County School District 14 has an employee assistance program. Provider: Lincoln Financial EmployeeConnect (services provided by ComPsych). Sessions: up to five sessions with a counselor per person, per issue, per year. Phone: 888-628-4824. Log in: GuidanceResources.com. Login details are in your district's benefits guide.

Who is covered: Employees and their dependents.

Login details are in your district's benefits guide.

Making the most of your sessions

  • Call the number, not just the website: the intake call is where you get matched.
  • Ask how sessions are counted, per issue or per year.
  • Write down what is hardest, what you have tried, and what "better" looks like by the last session.
