Does Mapleton Public Schools have an employee assistance program?

Yes. Mapleton Public Schools has an employee assistance program. Provider: AllOne Health (through CEBT). Sessions: 6 free counseling sessions per year. Phone: (877) 679-1100 or (970) 242-9536. Log in: www.allonehealth.com/portal, Company Code: cebt.

Who is covered: CEBT members and dependents under 26.

Also listed in the district's guide: The Standard (group life and disability) EAP: (888) 293-6948, workhealthlife.com/Standard3. Modern Health: 8 therapy + 8 coaching sessions per year for members/dependents 6+ on a medical plan.

Last verified November 27, 2026. Official sources:

Making the most of your sessions

  • Call the number, not just the website: the intake call is where you get matched.
  • Ask how sessions are counted, per issue or per year.
  • Write down what is hardest, what you have tried, and what "better" looks like by the last session.
