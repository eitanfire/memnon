Does Douglas County School District RE-1 have an employee assistance program?

Yes. Douglas County School District RE-1 has an employee assistance program. Provider: SupportLinc (CuraLinc). Sessions: up to eight professional, confidential virtual or face-to-face visits per year, per issue. Phone: 888-881-5462 (1-888-881-LINC). Log in: mysupportlinc.com, Group Code: dcsd. Login details are in your district's benefits guide.

Who is covered: Employees and household members (automatic access).

Making the most of your sessions

  • Call the number, not just the website: the intake call is where you get matched.
  • Ask how sessions are counted, per issue or per year.
  • Write down what is hardest, what you have tried, and what "better" looks like by the last session.
