Does Weld RE-5J School District (Johnstown-Milliken) have an employee assistance program?

Yes. Weld RE-5J School District (Johnstown-Milliken) has an employee assistance program. Provider: AllOne Health (through CEBT). Sessions: 6 free counseling sessions per year. Phone: (970) 242-9536 or (877) 679-1100. Log in: allonehealth.com/portal (Member Portal > Sign Up), Company Code: CEBT.

Who is covered: CEBT members and dependents under 26.

Also listed in the district's guide: Separate Modern Health mental health benefit for members and dependents ages 6+ enrolled in a medical plan: 8 therapy and 8 coaching sessions per year (my.modernhealth.com, company name CEBT).

Last verified November 27, 2026. Official sources:

  • weldre5j.org

Making the most of your sessions

  • Call the number, not just the website: the intake call is where you get matched.
  • Ask how sessions are counted, per issue or per year.
  • Write down what is hardest, what you have tried, and what "better" looks like by the last session.
