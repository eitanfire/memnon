Does School District 27J have an employee assistance program?

Yes. School District 27J has an employee assistance program. Provider: Resources For Living. Sessions: up to 5 counseling sessions per issue each year. Phone: 1-866-252-4468, TTY: 711. Log in: Resourcesforliving.com. Login details are in your district's benefits guide.

Who is covered: Employees and all members of the household, including dependent children up to 26 whether or not they live at home.

Login details are in your district's benefits guide.

Some of this comes from a past-year or benefits-trust guide, so confirm it with HR.

Making the most of your sessions

  • Call the number, not just the website: the intake call is where you get matched.
  • Ask how sessions are counted, per issue or per year.
  • Write down what is hardest, what you have tried, and what "better" looks like by the last session.
