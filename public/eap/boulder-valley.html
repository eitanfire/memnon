Does Boulder Valley School District RE-2 have an employee assistance program?

Yes. Boulder Valley School District RE-2 has an employee assistance program. Provider: ComPsych (GuidanceResources). Sessions: up to eight (8) free in-person or virtual sessions with a counselor per event, per year, per individual. Phone: 866-519-8359. Log in: www.guidanceresources.com (no company Web ID published in the guide).

Who is covered: Employees and household members; eligible (benefits-eligible, generally 0.5 FTE+) employees are automatically enrolled, paid 100% by BVSD.

Last verified November 27, 2026. Official sources:

Making the most of your sessions

  • Call the number, not just the website: the intake call is where you get matched.
  • Ask how sessions are counted, per issue or per year.
  • Write down what is hardest, what you have tried, and what "better" looks like by the last session.
