Colorado school district employee assistance programs

Most Colorado school districts give staff a few free, confidential counseling sessions through an employee assistance program (EAP). Find your district for the provider, the number of sessions, the phone number and how to log in.

  • Adams 12 Five Star Schools (Thornton): ComPsych (GuidanceResources)
  • Adams County School District 14 (Commerce City): Lincoln Financial EmployeeConnect (services provided by ComPsych)
  • Aurora Public Schools (Adams-Arapahoe 28J) (Aurora): ComPsych (GuidanceResources)
  • Boulder Valley School District RE-2 (Boulder): ComPsych (GuidanceResources)
  • Cherry Creek School District 5 (Greenwood Village): Mines & Associates
  • Denver Public Schools (Denver): We could not confirm this district's EAP from a public source; ask HR.
  • Douglas County School District RE-1 (Castle Rock): SupportLinc (CuraLinc)
  • Estes Park School District R-3 (Estes Park): We could not confirm this district's EAP from a public source; ask HR.
  • Greeley-Evans School District 6 (Greeley): SupportLinc (CuraLinc Healthcare), new provider as of July 1, 2026
  • Jeffco Public Schools (Jefferson County R-1) (Golden): Jeffco Public Schools Employee Assistance Program (district-run; EAP staff provide assessment, brief therapy and referral)
  • Littleton Public Schools (Littleton): We could not confirm this district's EAP from a public source; ask HR.
  • Mapleton Public Schools (Thornton): AllOne Health (through CEBT)
  • Poudre School District R-1 (Fort Collins): Employee Assistance Services (EAS), run by the district (in-house program)
  • School District 27J (Brighton): Resources For Living
  • St. Vrain Valley School District RE-1J (Longmont): ComPsych (GuidanceResources)
  • Thompson School District R2-J (Loveland): AllOne Health
  • Weld County School District RE-1 (Gilcrest): Depends on enrollment: Anthem EAP (Anthem medical members), Kaiser EAP (Kaiser members), Equitable EAP (employees in district-provided life insurance)
  • Weld RE-4 School District (Windsor-Severance) (Windsor): AllOne Health (through CEBT)
  • Weld RE-5J School District (Johnstown-Milliken) (Milliken): AllOne Health (through CEBT)
  • Westminster Public Schools (Westminster): Resources For Living

Colorado State Employee Assistance Program (C-SEAP), reference entry: Reference only. School district employees should use their district EAP. Family members may join couple/family sessions but cannot get individual C-SEAP counseling. cseap.colorado.gov

Every entry comes from the district's own pages or benefits guide. Last verified November 27, 2026.

Making the most of your sessions

  • Call the number, not just the website: the intake call is where you get matched.
  • Ask how sessions are counted, per issue or per year.
  • Write down what is hardest, what you have tried, and what "better" looks like by the last session.
