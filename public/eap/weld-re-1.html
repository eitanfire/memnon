Does Weld County School District RE-1 have an employee assistance program?

Yes. Weld County School District RE-1 has an employee assistance program. Provider: Depends on enrollment: Anthem EAP (Anthem medical members), Kaiser EAP (Kaiser members), Equitable EAP (employees in district-provided life insurance). Sessions: the district does not publish a limit; ask when you call. Phone: the district does not publish one; ask HR.

Who is covered: District page: 'all employees and their families'. 2026-27 guide ties each EAP to the carrier the employee is enrolled with.

Last verified November 27, 2026. Official sources:

  • drive.google.com

Making the most of your sessions

  • Call the number, not just the website: the intake call is where you get matched.
  • Ask how sessions are counted, per issue or per year.
  • Write down what is hardest, what you have tried, and what "better" looks like by the last session.
