Interview guide

Questions worth asking

These are aimed at uncovering problem intensity, current behavior, and whether Memnon is entering a meaningful workflow rather than sounding merely interesting.

Where does teaching feel hardest right now? Look for recurring emotional or cognitive load, not just logistics.
When a difficult day happens, what do you do with it? This reveals whether reflection already exists and in what medium.
What gets left unprocessed because there isn't time or privacy? Good for surfacing the case for text as well as voice.
What do you wish a reflective tool understood about your work automatically? This points at context, memory, and personalization needs.
If Memnon disappeared tomorrow, what would you miss most? This gets at actual value rather than polite approval.
What would make you stop using it after one week? This surfaces trust, friction, and workflow misalignment early.
