Add Memnon to Claude

Memnon works as a connector in Claude. Today it does one thing: ask Claude about your school district's employee assistance program and it answers from the Colorado EAP directory, with the provider, free sessions, phone number, how to log in and when it was last checked. No account or sign-in needed.

Set it up

  1. In Claude, open Customize, then Connectors.
  2. Choose Add custom connector.
  3. Name it Memnon and paste this URL: https://memnon.app/api/mcp
  4. Choose Add. No sign-in is needed.

If you don't see Add custom connector, your Claude plan or your workspace admin may not allow custom connectors yet.

Try asking

  • "How many free counseling sessions do St. Vrain teachers get?"
  • "What's the EAP phone number for Boulder Valley?"
  • "I teach in Thornton. Who runs my EAP?"

What Memnon can see

Only the public EAP directory. The connector can't read or change anything of yours, and it does not keep what you ask.
