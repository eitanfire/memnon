## Passage: What Is in Your Power
You have power over your mind, not outside events. Realize this, and you will find strength.

*Meditations, paraphrase of Book VI*

## Passage: The Present Moment
Confine yourself to the present.

*Meditations, 8.7*

## Passage: On Impermanence
Loss is nothing else but change, and change is Nature's delight.

*Meditations, 9.35*

## Passage: Time and Generations
Of human life the time is a point, and the substance is in a flux, and the perception dull, and the composition of the whole body subject to putrefaction, and the soul a whirl, and fortune hard to divine, and fame a thing devoid of judgment. All things of the body stream away like a river, all things of the mind are dreams and delusion.

*Meditations, 2.17*

## Passage: The Obstacle Is the Way
The impediment to action advances action. What stands in the way becomes the way.

*Meditations, 5.20*

## Passage: On Our Common Nature
We are made for cooperation, like feet, like hands, like eyelids, like the rows of the upper and lower teeth. To act against one another is contrary to nature.

*Meditations, 2.1*

## Passage: Begin the Morning
When you wake in the morning, tell yourself: the people I deal with today will be meddling, ungrateful, arrogant, dishonest, jealous, and surly. They are this way because they cannot tell good from evil. But I have seen the beauty of good and the ugliness of evil, and I recognize that the wrongdoer has a nature related to my own.

*Meditations, 2.1*

## Passage: Look Within
Nowhere can man find a quieter or more untroubled retreat than in his own soul.

*Meditations, 4.3*
